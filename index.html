Why Stacked exists

Because a burger should be messy for the right reasons.

Stacked is built around hot griddles, crispy edges and short menus. No towering novelty burgers. No dry brioche. No pretending a knife through the middle helps.

We keep the menu focused, cook everything to order and move around Cork throughout the week.
