Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains how PopBia handles information when you use our website, create an account or use PopBia Collection. For privacy questions, email hello@popbia.com.

Information we process

  • Accounts: your email address, account identifier and authentication/session information. Email/password authentication is handled through Supabase.
  • Your business: workspace name, membership and account links, Collection display settings, and trial/access status and dates.
  • Collection orders: order numbers, optional customer names, preparation/ready/collected status, and creation, ready and collection timestamps. Collected orders can remain in the workspace history.
  • Enquiries: information you send us by email, such as your name, email, business details, demo or paid-access interest and message. Email links open your email app; opening a draft does not send it to PopBia. Demo/template forms may prepare an email draft with the details you enter.
  • Technical information: hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, request/device information and operational logs to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the service. Cloudflare observability is enabled in the project configuration; the exact hosted log settings and retention depend on the provider configuration.

Why we use it

We use this information to create and maintain accounts, operate business workspaces, show order progress, manage trial access, respond to enquiries and support requests, and keep the service working securely. Account information is needed to provide account-based Collection access; customer names are optional.

PopBia does not sell personal information. We do not use the current site for advertising tracking or behavioural analytics.

Customer information and the display board

Businesses decide what customer information to enter and are responsible for explaining that use to their customers. PopBia processes that information to provide Collection for the business. Active order numbers and names, where entered, can appear on the customer-facing board and be viewed by anyone with its display link. Avoid entering sensitive information or more identifying detail than needed for collection.

Service providers and browser storage

Supabase provides account authentication, database storage and live Collection updates. Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the network information needed to serve those requests. Email enquiries pass through your email provider and the provider used for PopBia’s inbox.

Supabase stores account session information in your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in. The current site does not include analytics or advertising cookies. Provider systems may process information in locations outside your country; contact us if you need details about the arrangements for your business.

The website currently uses direct email links for PopBia enquiries; it does not submit them through a website email-sending service.

How long information is kept

Account and workspace information is kept to support your use of the service. Trial expiry restricts access; it does not automatically delete your workspace or order history. The current product does not implement a fixed automatic retention period for accounts or collected orders.

Enquiries may be retained to respond and keep relevant correspondence. When considering a deletion request, we take account of whether information is still needed to provide the service, resolve an issue or meet an applicable legal obligation. Provider logs and backups may remain under their own retention arrangements; deletion from the live service may not immediately remove every backup copy.

Your choices and requests

Email hello@popbia.com to request access to, correction of or deletion of your information. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing and receive a portable copy. We may need to verify your identity and understand which account or business is involved.

If your information was entered by a business using Collection, contact that business first so it can handle your request; we can help it with the information held in PopBia. You may also raise a concern with your local data protection authority, including Ireland’s Data Protection Commission.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the early-stage product develops. The date above shows the latest revision. Contact PopBia at hello@popbia.com for privacy questions.
