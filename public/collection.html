The service flow

Three taps from order to handover.

  1. 1

    Add the order

    Every order gets an automatic number. Staff may optionally add the customer’s name.

  2. 2

    Mark it Ready

    When the order is finished, staff tap Ready.

  3. 3

    Customer collects

    The display updates, then staff mark the order Collected when it is handed over.
