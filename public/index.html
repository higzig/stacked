    A few useful answers

    Before you get started.

    What hardware do I need?

    A phone or tablet browser for staff controls, and a browser on a screen or customers’ phones for the customer board. No special hardware needed.

    Does it replace my till/register?

    No. Collection works alongside your existing register or ordering process to manage order status and handover.

    Can customers use their own phones?

    Yes. A QR code opens your customer board on their phone so they can see Preparing and Ready orders.

    How long does setup take?

    Setup takes no more than 30 minutes.
